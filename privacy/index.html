Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Who we are

DuckyHug (“we”) is a small family business that writes and publishes children's books. This site is at www.duckyhug.com.

What we collect

If you join our list, we collect your email address (and your first name, if a form asks for it). We do not ask for anything else.

Why we collect it

To send you the free printable kit you asked for, and to send occasional news about our books. Nothing else.

Who handles it

We use Kit (kit.com) to store your email address and to send our emails. Kit processes this data on our behalf.

Unsubscribe or delete your data

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the bottom. To have your data deleted, message us on Facebook and we will remove it.

Children

Our sign-up forms are for parents and guardians only. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (US) or under 16 in the EU without a parent's consent. If you think a child has given us their email address, tell us and we will delete it.
